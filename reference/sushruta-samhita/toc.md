# Sushruta Samhita (सुश्रुतसंहिता)

- Author / compiler: Suśruta (teacher: Dhanvantari, king of Kashi, named in the opening of the text)
- Subject and purpose of the book: the classical Sanskrit text of surgery and medicine; chapter 1 opens with the sages asking Dhanvantari to teach Ayurveda, and he agrees to teach it with surgery (śalya) as its base (stated in the text, 01.01.3 to 01.01.11)
- Base edition: Yādavaśarman Trivikramātmaja Ācārya, Bombay: Pāṇḍurang Jāvaji, 1931 (Sūtrasthāna, revised 2nd ed.) and 1938 (later sthānas); SARIT e-text by Tsutomu Yamashita and Yasutaka Muroya, commentary omitted (source S1 in sources.md)
- Mapping: part = sthāna (01 Sūtra to 06 Uttara), chapter = adhyāya, verse = verse or prose sūtra as numbered in the source (`Su.S.C.V`); see FORMAT.md
- Totals: 6 parts, 185 chapters, 8282 verses (distinct verse numbers) extracted (traditional: 186 chapters; this e-text has 185, Śārīrasthāna ends at chapter 9)

Layout: Sūtrasthāna, Cikitsāsthāna, Kalpasthāna and Uttaratantra have one file per chapter; Nidānasthāna and Śārīrasthāna are each under 1000 lines and are one file per part (`p02.md`, `p03.md`). Chapter 01.46 has 1032 lines and is kept whole.

Chapter names are read from each chapter's opening formula ("athāto ... adhyāyaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ") by script and converted to Devanagari; they are not checked against another edition. Purpose lines are glosses of the title and are marked (inferred).
